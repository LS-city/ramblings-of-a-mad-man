# Ramblings of a Mad Man

By Edwin C. Reyes.

An interactive edition of the book: open the cover, turn the pages, jump around with the table of contents, bookmark pages, pick up where you left off, and write your own pages at the end.

## Read it

Live at **https://ls-city.github.io/ramblings-of-a-mad-man/**

## Adding a chapter

Open `chapters.txt`, click the pencil icon to edit, and add your chapter at the bottom:

```
# Chapter Title

First paragraph.

( a line in the second voice )
```

Commit the change. The live book updates within a minute or two. New chapters land after "A Terrible Mind" and before "Ramblings of a Patch", and the chapter numbers and contents page update on their own.

## Files

- `index.html` is the whole book: text, design, and page-turning.
- `chapters.txt` holds chapters added after launch.

Readers' bookmarks and "Write Here" pages save in their own browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
