HOW TO ADD A CHAPTER
Everything above the first chapter is ignored, so these notes never show in the book.

Start each new chapter with a line that begins with "# " followed by the title.
Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
Start a paragraph with ( for the second voice (red italics).
A line with *** makes a scene break.
Short lines kept together (no blank line between them) come out as a poem.

New chapters appear after "A Terrible Mind" and before "Ramblings of a Patch",
in the order you write them here. Chapter numbers update on their own.

Example (remove the two spaces before the # to make it real):

  # Night Shift

  The register hums.

  ( you know you love it here )

Add your chapters below this line.
